Privacy Policy

Sprinkle respects your privacy. This page describes the basic information a visitor may provide when joining the newsletter or contacting the brand.

Information you share

If you sign up for The Little Things Club, Sprinkle may collect your email address and any optional information you choose to provide, such as your first name.

How information is used

Information submitted through the newsletter form may be used to send editorial updates, lifestyle inspiration, and brand news. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link included in those emails.

Third-party services

Sprinkle may use a third-party email service provider, such as Klaviyo, to manage newsletter signups and email delivery. Please review that provider's policies for additional details about how form submissions are processed.
